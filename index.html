Reading these numbers. Award records are not founders: 141 awards resolve to 126 distinct founders, because founders recur. Survival is counted once per founder from their most recent participation year. Sprint capital is the Sprint programme only and is never the same figure as total capital deployed.

Joins. Email is the only key connecting any two sources. Populations are reported separately rather than merged, because they overlap poorly: the census shares only 41 addresses with the grants table. Any chart that looks like a join is labelled as one.

Privacy. Aggregates and a de-identified award table only. No name, email, business name, phone or address is present. Founder ids are sequential surrogates assigned at build time. Demographics are aggregate and deliberately not filterable, because at cohort sizes of four a race by city filter can identify a person. The capital award payment survey is excluded from this product entirely by policy.
